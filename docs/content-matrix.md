# Content Matrix specification

The product combines three ideas while treating none as a universal law:

- The original Content Marketing Matrix contributes audience journey and emotional/rational variation.
- See-Think-Do-Care contributes audience intent states and measurement alignment.
- Maximal Marginal Relevance contributes explicit relevance/diversity portfolio selection.

## Dimensions

Core dimensions are audience segment, job/pain/gain, intent stage, pillar, objective, Instagram surface, format, angle, evidence type, narrative mechanism, CTA, lifecycle, effort, and risk. Accounts may extend the taxonomy.

## Why it is not a grid

Multiplying every dimension produces many invalid or strategically redundant cells. The engine accepts feasible candidates, validates required dimensions, scores each one, enforces concentration/coverage constraints, and greedily selects a diverse portfolio using MMR.

## Scoring boundary

Initial weights are product defaults, not platform facts. Every selection stores base relevance, redundancy, coverage bonus, constraints, and unmet targets. Calibration uses account-specific experiments and should retain a versioned profile.
