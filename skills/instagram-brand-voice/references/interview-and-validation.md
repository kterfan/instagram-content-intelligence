# Brand Voice Interview and Validation

## Interview evidence order

1. Reuse existing verified account material.
2. Ask identity and positioning questions that change wording decisions.
3. Use 0–1 scales for directness, warmth, humor, authority, and emotional intensity, followed by one concrete example at the chosen level.
4. Collect at least three approved samples across relevant formats and two rejected/contrast samples.
5. Ask the owner to explain why each contrast is wrong; convert reasons into observable rules rather than more adjectives.

## Voice DNA boundaries

- `owner intent`: desired personality and audience relationship.
- `observed`: lexical, rhythm, punctuation, emoji, CTA, and code-switching patterns from approved samples.
- `preference`: explicit rules even when the sample set is small.
- `performance`: correlations from content results; never causal by default.
- `gap`: missing evidence that another skill must not silently fill.

## Blind validation

Hide candidate labels. Ask the owner to select the most authentic candidate and cite specific cues. A useful acceptance round includes an approved or human-written control, a DNA-guided candidate, and a fluent generic candidate. Record selection accuracy, reasons, and corrections. Do not optimize only for automatic heuristic score.

## Drift

When new approved samples materially change observed sentence length, vocabulary, punctuation, CTA behavior, or format variants, create a new DNA version. Preserve the old version and record why the change was accepted.
